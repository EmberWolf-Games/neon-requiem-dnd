This derivative excludes the upstream `tokens/` artwork licensed for use only with the official dnd5e system, Caeora composite tokens, Wizards of the Coast school and official UI art, spell-tier images with separate restrictions, currency images without a clear license in the source tree, and other UI artwork without a separate license file. Do not reintroduce these assets without obtaining and recording rights that cover Neon Requiem.
