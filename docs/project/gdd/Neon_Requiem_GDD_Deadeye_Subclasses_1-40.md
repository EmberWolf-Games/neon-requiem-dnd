# NEON REQUIEM — Deadeye Subclasses (Levels 1–40)

**Decision status:** OWNER-PRE-APPROVED SUBCLASS DESIGN DRAFT — editable in later review and playtesting  
**Date:** 2026-11-21  
**Base class:** Deadeye ([approved 1–40 progression](Neon_Requiem_GDD_Deadeye_Progression_1-40.md))  
**Implementation:** NOT IMPLEMENTED. This document is not permission to make gameplay changes or publish a build.

## Rules shared by all Deadeye subclasses

- Every subclass advances at Deadeye class levels **3, 7, 11, 15, 19, 23, 27, 31, 35, 39**.
- **Deadeye feature DC (provisional):** 8 + proficiency bonus + DEX modifier; 21–40 proficiency progression remains to be defined system-wide.
- **Focus Dice** remain the Deadeye base-class pool (4d6 at Deadeye 2; 5d8 at 13; 6d10 at 21; 7d12 at 30). Spending a die for a subclass technique obeys the base class's restriction of **at most one spent Focus Die per attack** unless an explicit approved exception says otherwise.
- No subclass grants free ammunition, explosives, gun manufacture, unlimited attacks, or magic. **Actual firearm stats, ammo, magazines, reload and heavy weapon properties** are still to be standardized.
- ASI/feat choices remain at Deadeye **4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 28, 32, 36, 40**. Subclass milestones do not create extra feat levels.

## Longshot

**Identity:** Stationary precision, weak-point targeting, patient ranged reconnaissance and carefully chosen elimination shots.

| Deadeye level | Feature | Initial playtest mechanic |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 3 | **Sniper's Perch** | Once per turn, if you have not moved this turn and hit a target at least 30 feet away with a firearm, add damage equal to your DEX modifier (minimum 1). You must have a clear firing lane. |
| 7 | **Calculated Range** | When making one firearm attack at long range, expend a Focus Die to negate disadvantage caused solely by that range. The die may also fuel one normal Shot Technique on that attack, but cannot be spent twice. |
| 11 | **Deliberate Aim** | Once per Short Rest, use a Bonus Action to study a visible target while you remain stationary; gain advantage on your next firearm attack against that target before the end of your next turn. |
| 15 | **Cover Geometry** | For one firearm attack on your turn against a target in three-quarters cover, treat that cover as half cover if you have line of sight and did not move before the attack. This cannot defeat total cover. |
| 19 | **Opening Volley** | On your first turn of combat, the first firearm hit you land on your Target Locked enemy deals one extra weapon damage die. Once per encounter. |
| 23 | **Nerve Shot** | When you hit your Target Locked foe with a firearm attack, expend a Focus Die to force a CON save against your class feature DC; on failure the creature's speed is halved until the start of your next turn. Once per turn. |
| 27 | **Predator's Sightline** | Once per turn, if your Target Locked enemy is at or below half its maximum HP and at least 30 feet away, your first firearm attack against it scores a critical hit on a 19 or 20. |
| 31 | **Silent Reposition** | After a successful firearm hit from behind substantial cover, you may move up to 10 feet to another legal position without provoking an opportunity attack from that target; once per turn. |
| 35 | **Perfect Trajectory** | Once per Short Rest, after making a firearm attack against your Target Locked target from a stationary firing position, reroll a miss. You must accept the second roll and still obey range, line of fire and cover. |
| 39 | **Legendary Marksman** | Once per Long Rest, when you hit a Target Locked creature at least 30 feet away, maximize one weapon damage die and one Focus Die already applied to that attack. You cannot grant a second Focus Die to the same shot. |

## Gunslinger

**Identity:** Mobile close-quarters shooting, rapid transitions, one-handed firearms, and tactical footwork.

| Deadeye level | Feature | Initial playtest mechanic |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 3 | **Quickdraw** | Add your proficiency bonus to Initiative if you begin combat with an accessible loaded one-handed firearm. Drawing one one-handed firearm at the start of your first turn does not consume your ordinary interaction. |
| 7 | **Close Quarters Gunplay** | Your ranged firearm attacks made using eligible compact/one-handed firearms do not suffer disadvantage solely because a hostile creature is within 5 feet of you. |
| 11 | **Run and Gun** | Once on each of your turns after hitting a creature with a compact firearm attack, move up to 10 feet without provoking opportunity attacks from that creature. |
| 15 | **Paired Fire** | When you take the Attack action with an eligible loaded one-handed firearm and hold a second compatible loaded firearm, you may use a Bonus Action to fire the second once. Standard ammunition, weapon access, reload and occupied-hand limits still apply. |
| 19 | **Split Pressure** | Once per turn when you successfully fire at one enemy, spend a Focus Die to force a second visible enemy within 10 feet of the first to make a WIS save; on failure, the second target has disadvantage on its next attack roll before your next turn. This is suppressive pressure, not mental control. |
| 23 | **Moving Target** | After moving at least 10 feet on your turn, you gain +2 AC against the first ranged weapon attack that targets you before the start of your next turn. This benefit is once per round. |
| 27 | **Combat Reload Mastery** | Once per Short Rest, incorporate one normally eligible firearm reload into your Attack action without consuming an additional action. This does not create ammunition or bypass a weapon's physical capacity. |
| 31 | **Running Shot** | When you use Dash as your action, you may fire one loaded compact firearm as a Bonus Action. This is one shot and does not also trigger Extra Attack or Paired Fire. |
| 35 | **Bullet Ballet** | When Paired Fire hits, you may move 5 feet without provoking opportunity attacks from the creature hit by that attack. This movement occurs after the shot and no more than once per turn. |
| 39 | **Gunslinger's Flourish** | Once per Short Rest, after hitting with a compact firearm on your turn, make one additional compact firearm attack against a *different* visible target within the weapon's normal range as part of that Attack action. It consumes ammunition and cannot trigger itself. |

## Demolitionist

**Identity:** Heavy ordnance, controlled demolition, suppressive fire, breaching and terrain denial.

| Deadeye level | Feature | Initial playtest mechanic |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 3 | **Ordnance Handling** | Gain proficiency with demolition equipment; when you attack with a trained heavy firearm or use a prepared explosive, you can expend a Focus Die to apply a suppression Shot Technique. You must have the actual weapon, ammunition and explosives. |
| 7 | **Controlled Blast** | When using an explosive with an area effect, choose one ally you can see in that area. That ally gains advantage on its saving throw against that specific explosion; the effect is not automatic friendly-fire immunity. |
| 11 | **Stabilized Weapon Platform** | When braced or supported by suitable equipment, you ignore the ordinary movement penalty of a heavy firearm (if one exists). You do not bypass weapon proficiency, required Strength, weight or recoil limitations. |
| 15 | **Fragmentation Control** | Once on your turn when an explosive damages one or more enemies, choose one damaged target and increase its damage taken by your proficiency bonus. Only if you deployed that explosive yourself. |
| 19 | **Suppression Corridor** | Once per Short Rest, use your action, appropriate ammunition and a Focus Die to establish a 20-foot-long, 5-foot-wide fire lane within weapon range until your next turn. A creature that voluntarily crosses it makes a WIS save or has its movement halved that turn; the lane itself deals no extra automatic damage. |
| 23 | **Breach Specialist** | When using a real breaching charge on a mundane unattended barrier, ignore a portion of its damage threshold equal to your proficiency bonus. This does not bypass magical/technical locks, total cover, or living targets' defenses. |
| 27 | **Blast Calibration** | When deploying an explosive you prepared, adjust its blast origin by up to 5 feet within its legal placement area, without increasing its normal radius. One chosen ally gains +2 on the associated saving throw. |
| 31 | **Shrapnel Suppression** | Once per Short Rest, when your explosive deals damage, force one affected creature to make a CON save; on failure its speed decreases by 10 feet and it cannot take Reactions until the start of its next turn. |
| 35 | **Targeted Detonation** | Once per Short Rest, after rolling the damage for an explosive you deployed, reroll up to four of its damage dice and use the new results. You may not apply this to a nonexistent or unarmed explosive. |
| 39 | **Artillery Dominance** | Once per Long Rest, when you successfully hit with a heavy firearm or detonate your prepared explosive, designate a 10-foot-radius suppression zone around the impact until your next turn; enemies entering the zone have their speed halved. The original attack/blast damage and area rules still apply. |

## Open balance / implementation questions

- Define eligible compact firearms, sniper weapons, heavy firearms and explosive item templates before enforcing the subclasses.
- Once standard firearm and magazine rules exist, resolve Paired Fire, reloading, extra shots and ammunition expenditure against the universal action economy.
- Longshot gains strong single-target advantages, Gunslinger conditional extra attacks and mobility, Demolitionist more suppression and area denial. Preserve those identities through 40-level playtests.
- All 30 named milestones are **pre-approved concepts**, not finalized damage, save, or equipment specifications.
