# Deadeye — Base Class Progression (Levels 1–40)

**Status:** Approved design; numerical balance pending  
**Recorded:** 21 Nov 2026  
**Subclasses:** See [Deadeye subclass features](Neon_Requiem_GDD_Deadeye_Subclasses_1-40.md)  
**Build status:** Design only; no game-system implementation yet.

## 1. Class foundation

**Deadeye** is a specialist in firearms, precision, ranged positioning, target acquisition, suppression, and battlefield firepower. The main 5e reference points are Fighter's martial reliability and fighting styles, Ranger's focused-target approach, and Battle Master-like tactical maneuvers. These are mechanical inspirations, not copied spellcasting.

The accepted draft proposes:

- **Hit Die:** d10.
- **Primary ability:** Dexterity.
- **Secondary ability:** Wisdom.
- **Saving throw proficiencies (proposed):** DEX and WIS. **Balance flag:** These are both strong 5e saves; reassess the pairing before finalizing the class chassis.
- **Armor:** Light and medium.
- **Weapons:** Simple weapons and all conventional firearms.
- **Main combat resource:** Focus Dice, spent to power Shot Techniques.
- **Core identity:** Win through *where, when, and how* attacks are made, not a high number of ordinary attacks.

The three subclass identities are **Longshot** (sniping/long-range precision), **Gunslinger** (mobile/fast gunplay), and **Demolitionist** (heavy weapons/explosives/suppressive fire).

## 4. Conceptual base-class features from the accepted proposal

### Levels 1–10

- **Marksmanship Discipline (1):** Choose a shooting discipline: **Precision** (aimed attacks), **Skirmisher** (mobile gunplay), or **Suppressor** (restricting enemy movement through covering fire). These are *base-class disciplines*, not subclasses.
- **Threat Assessment (1):** Improved battlefield awareness, including proficiency in Perception or Investigation and a bonus to identifying useful firing positions.
- **Focus Dice / Shot Techniques (2):** Start with **four d6 Focus Dice**, restored after a Short or Long Rest. Learn **two** initial techniques, for example accuracy aid, movement interference, or creating an opening for an ally. Only **one spent Focus Die may benefit a given weapon attack**, unless later explicitly overridden.
- **Extra Attack (5):** Two attacks with the Attack action.
- **Combat Reload (6):** Increased efficiency drawing, swapping, and reloading firearms. The exact function awaits universal magazine/ammunition rules.
- **Target Lock (9):** As a Bonus Action, mark one visible enemy within firearm range. Against that target, a modest tactical benefit (accepted example: reroll one weapon damage die on the first successful hit each turn). Only one Target Lock active at a time.
- **Cover Sense (10):** While making a suitably positioned firearm attack, partially negate the protection of half cover.

### Levels 11–20

- **Improved Focus (13):** Focus pool becomes **5d8**.
- **Steady Nerves (14):** Improved resistance to fear and maintaining composure under enemy fire.
- **Target Lock II (17):** After defeating a locked enemy, designate a new target without using another Bonus Action.
- **Evasive Fire (18):** Spend a Focus Die and Reaction to enhance defense or reposition while keeping a weapon trained on the threat.

### Levels 21–30

- **Elite Focus (21):** Focus pool becomes **6d10**.
- **Tactical Relocation (22):** Improved movement after making ranged attacks, supporting repositioning and disengagement.
- **Extra Attack (25):** Three attacks with the Attack action.
- **Surgical Shot (26):** Spend a Focus Die for a tactical vulnerability effect, such as impairing mobility, disrupting an action, or granting an advantage to the squad. Exact menu and saves TBD.
- **Rolling Barrage (29):** Once on each turn, when a firearm attack scores a critical hit or reduces a hostile creature to 0 HP, make an additional firearm attack as a Bonus Action.
- **Focus Mastery (30):** Focus pool becomes **7d12**.

### Levels 31–40

- **Zero In (33):** Missing a Target Locked foe improves the next attempt to hit it before the end of the next turn; adjust aim instead of accumulating indefinite bonuses.
- **Counterfire (34):** Spend a Focus Die and Reaction to shoot at a visible attacker targeting an ally within effective firearm range.
- **Unbroken Line (37):** Improved extreme-range accuracy against the locked target; cannot shoot through solid obstacles or total cover.
- **Deadeye's Instinct (38):** At Initiative, recover **two Focus Dice** if no Focus Dice remain.
- **Perfect Shot (40):** Once per Long Rest, enter extraordinary precision for **up to one minute**. While active: once per turn, apply a Focus Die shooting technique without expending the die; the first firearm attack each turn ignores half and three-quarters cover; long-range attacks against the locked target do not suffer disadvantage *solely* from range. Does **not** guarantee a hit or bypass total cover/line-of-fire requirements.

## 5. Reserved design slots and balance boundaries

### ASI/feat and subclass cadence

- **10 ASI/feat choices:** levels **4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 28, 32, 36, 40**.
- **10 subclass milestones:** levels **3, 7, 11, 15, 19, 23, 27, 31, 35, 39**.
- Longshot, Gunslinger and Demolitionist now have full draft feature tables. Their exact numerical tuning is still open.

### Focus Dice scaling

| Deadeye level | Resource pool |
| ---: | --- |
| 2 | **4d6** |
| 13 | **5d8** |
| 21 | **6d10** |
| 30 | **7d12** |

- Focus Dice return after a **Short or Long Rest** in the accepted draft.
- Final technique menus, usage restrictions, damage and DC math, and resource balance require dedicated review and playtesting.

### Weapon combat constraints

- Deadeye gets **two Attack-action attacks at level 5** and **three at level 25**. No fourth ordinary Attack-action attack through level 40 is proposed.
- **Vanguard is distinct:** it gets its third attack at level 13 and fourth at level 29. Deadeye specializes in high-value precision and techniques, not copying Vanguard's attack count.
- A bonus attack from Rolling Barrage is conditional, uses the Bonus Action, and is **not** the Extra Attack feature.
- Firearms, magazines, ammunition, reload costs, armor penetration, burst fire, and damage are not yet system-wide-approved. **Combat Reload and related features must integrate with those future rules.**
- System-wide 21–40 proficiency bonuses, ability-score caps, feat rules, and multiclass mechanics remain unresolved. Do not silently extend 5e's level 1–20 math.

## Balance review

Test Focus Dice and conditional Bonus Action shots against the Vanguard's higher attack count, the Ghost's Precision Strike and the finalized firearm rules. The three Deadeye subclass tracks are recorded separately. This remains a design specification, not a shipped feature.
