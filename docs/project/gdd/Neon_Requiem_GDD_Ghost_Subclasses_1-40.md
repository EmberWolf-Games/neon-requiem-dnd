# Ghost — Subclass Features (Levels 1–40)

**Status:** Pre-approved playtest draft; values may change  
**Recorded:** 21 Nov 2026  
**Base class:** [Ghost progression](Neon_Requiem_GDD_Ghost_Progression_1-40.md)  
**Build status:** Design only; not yet implemented.

Each Ghost takes a different route through a secured location. Phantom relies on cover and deception; Blade Dancer fights through movement; Saboteur prepares the ground before trouble starts.

## Rules that apply to every path

- Subclass milestones: Ghost class levels **3, 7, 11, 15, 19, 23, 27, 31, 35, 39**. Base-class ASI/feat selections remain at **4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 28, 32, 36, 40**.
- **Subclass feature DC (provisional):** 8 + proficiency bonus + DEX modifier for combat moves; trap/construction checks may use INT instead where explicitly noted. Final Ghost DC conventions to be unified with the common 1–40 system.
- Precision Strike's bonus damage remains **once per turn on one qualifying attack**. Subclass follow-up attacks do not double Precision Strike on that turn.
- No innate invisibility, teleportation, cybernetic effects, infinite traps or unauthorized digital access. Traps require suitable equipment/materials; electronic intrusion still needs a learned installed Hack and compatible Cyberware.
- Conditions, restraint, prone, opportunity attacks, stealth visibility, ammunition and tool item templates require integration with the actual Foundry/5e rules engine.

## Phantom

Phantom is about passing unnoticed, making one decisive move and leaving little for pursuers to follow.


#### Shadowcraft (Level 3)

Gain proficiency with disguise tools; if already proficient, gain Expertise. Once per Short Rest you may attempt Hide while lightly obscured by physical structures or a crowd, subject to the usual Stealth check and observer senses.

#### False Trail (Level 7)

After a successful Hide or disguise check, gain advantage on one check to mislead physical pursuit or conceal evidence of passage before the end of the next ten minutes. Does not erase digital logs automatically.

#### Silent Takedown (Level 11)

When you reduce a creature to 0 HP with a melee Precision Strike, you can choose nonlethal incapacitation if the weapon/target allows it, and immediately control its fall to prevent ordinary impact noise. Electronic distress signals remain possible.

#### Shadow Route (Level 15)

After successfully Hiding, your next 10 feet of movement before the end of your turn ignores nonhazardous difficult terrain from clutter. You remain subject to line of sight, noise and normal detection.

#### Clean Exit (Level 19)

Once per Short Rest after landing Precision Strike, you may move up to half your speed and attempt Hide as part of that movement without spending a Bonus Action, if you end in a position where Hiding is possible. This does not make you invisible.

#### Ghost Identity (Level 23)

With time, a disguise kit and appropriate credentials you prepared, gain advantage on one Deception or Performance check to pass as an invented local identity. Once per Short Rest; electronic authentication must still be handled legitimately or hacked with compatible software.

#### Quiet Capture (Level 27)

When you make a nonlethal melee Precision Strike on a creature eligible for physical restraint, you may forgo 2d6 damage to impose disadvantage on its next attempt to call out or break free before your next turn. It can still use electronic communication if able.

#### Blended Approach (Level 31)

Once per Short Rest, if you approach a creature while plausibly disguised and have not been identified as hostile, gain advantage on one Initiative roll or Dexterity (Stealth) check to begin the encounter. This cannot be used against a creature already tracking you.

#### Smoke and Mirrors (Level 35)

Once per Long Rest after dealing Precision Strike damage, move up to half your speed and attempt Hide without expending your Bonus Action. You must have actual cover or concealment; this feature never creates either.

#### Vanishing Operative (Level 39)

Once per Long Rest when a creature first detects you, use your Reaction to move up to your speed toward available concealment and attempt Hide if a legal location is reached. Movement can provoke opportunity attacks from other creatures.


## Blade Dancer

Blade Dancer specializes in finesse melee fighting. Movement and reactions are its defenses; it cannot take the same punishment as a Vanguard.


#### Flowing Guard (Level 3)

While using a finesse melee weapon and wearing no more than light armor, gain +1 AC against melee attacks if not using a shield. No bonus when incapacitated.

#### Circling Step (Level 7)

Once per turn after you hit a creature with a finesse melee weapon, move 5 feet without provoking opportunity attacks from that creature.

#### Riposte (Level 11)

Once per Short Rest when a creature misses you with a melee attack, use your Reaction to make one finesse melee weapon attack against it if it remains within reach. Precision Strike still obeys its once-per-turn and eligibility limits.

#### Second Tempo (Level 15)

Once on your turn after you miss with a finesse melee attack, make one additional finesse melee attack as a Bonus Action. This competes with Cunning Action and other Bonus Actions.

#### Deflective Parry (Level 19)

When you use Uncanny Dodge against a melee attack while holding a finesse weapon, further reduce the resulting damage by your DEX modifier (minimum 1). The same Reaction powers both effects.

#### Rapid Traverse (Level 23)

Your walking speed increases by 5 feet. When you Dash, you can move through a nonhostile creature's space without counting that space as difficult terrain; do not end movement there.

#### Sweeping Cut (Level 27)

Once per turn when you apply Precision Strike with a melee weapon, you can forgo 2d6 of its damage to force the target to make a STR save against your class feature DC. On failure it falls prone if physically possible.

#### Duelist's Momentum (Level 31)

Once per turn after moving at least 10 feet before a successful finesse melee hit, deal extra damage equal to your proficiency bonus.

#### Whirlwind Lunge (Level 35)

Once per Short Rest after landing a melee Precision Strike, use a Bonus Action to make one finesse melee attack against a *different* creature within reach. Precision Strike cannot be applied a second time on your turn.

#### Blade Dancer Mastery (Level 39)

Once per Long Rest after succeeding on a Dexterity saving throw against an area effect, use your Reaction to move up to 10 feet and make one finesse melee attack against a creature you can reach. The movement requires a legal path and may provoke opportunity attacks.


## Saboteur

Saboteur is strongest when given time to prepare. It bypasses physical security and turns the environment against intruders, but still needs tools and materials.


#### Trapwright (Level 3)

Gain proficiency in trapmaking/tinkering tools. With supplies, use an action to place one simple physical snare in an adjacent legal 5-foot space, once per Short Rest; only one such snare active at a time. A triggering creature makes a DEX save or is restrained until the end of its next turn (or escapes sooner using an action and appropriate check). The snare is visible to careful inspection and destroyable.

#### Quick Tamper (Level 7)

Once per Short Rest, use a Bonus Action instead of an action to work on an accessible mundane lock or mechanical trap with the appropriate tools. A normal check and physical access still apply. Does not constitute a Hack.

#### Distraction Charge (Level 11)

Once per Short Rest, place or throw an actual prepared noise/flash distraction device within its legitimate range. Creatures within 5 feet of it make a CON save or cannot take Reactions until the start of your next turn. Device materials are consumed.

#### Barbed Trap (Level 15)

When a trap you built damages a creature, it deals extra physical damage equal to your proficiency bonus once per trap activation. Your simple snare may be prepared with a damage-producing mechanism if materials allow; exact snare damage requires trap stats.

#### Linked Tampering (Level 19)

Once per Short Rest, work on two adjacent or mechanically linked physical security components as part of one action, rolling the necessary tool check separately for each. Digital networks and remote electronic locks still require proper Hacks.

#### Remote Trigger (Level 23)

With a functioning remote detonator/signal device and a compatible trap you personally set, use a Bonus Action to manually activate that trap within its supported 30-foot signal range. Signals may be jammed or intercepted; this is not unauthorized system control.

#### Tripwire Punishment (Level 27)

Once per Short Rest when a visible creature within 30 feet triggers a compatible physical trap you built, use your Reaction to force a DEX save; on failure it falls prone if the device can physically trip it. The trap still resolves its own ordinary effect.

#### Invisible Damage (Level 31)

Once per Long Rest when you successfully sabotage an unattended nonliving device using suitable tools, you may conceal the damage from casual visual inspection for up to one minute. A thorough technical inspection or appropriate sensor may reveal it.

#### Prepared Network (Level 35)

You may maintain two of your class-created simple traps simultaneously, provided you have legal space and the necessary materials. A Short Rest restores your one free Trapwright preparation but does not replace consumed devices automatically.

#### Cascade Detonation (Level 39)

Once per Long Rest, use one Bonus Action to activate two distinct compatible traps you previously placed and can legally trigger remotely. Each trap resolves separately and consumes its normal physical payload; no new traps or unlimited area effects appear.


## Balance review

- Phantom's cover/Hide features must never bypass active line-of-sight or electronic security with no Hack.
- Blade Dancer's Bonus Action attacks compete with Cunning Action, and added attacks cannot apply a second on-turn Precision Strike.
- Saboteur's simple traps should be useful but distinctly smaller than the Engineer's class-powered prepared gadgets; preserve placement, materials, detection, destruction and physical access constraints.
- These **30 subclass milestone concepts are owner-pre-approved** for the current design cycle and remain open to revisions.
