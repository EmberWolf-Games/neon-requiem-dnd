## 1. Class foundation

**Ghost** is a stealth, infiltration, agility, mobility, and precision specialist. The conceptual role combines official 5e Rogue's Sneak Attack, Expertise, Cunning Action, Evasion, and Uncanny Dodge with some Monk-inspired mobility, **without traditional magic or supernatural invisibility**.

- **Hit Die:** d8 (approved progression proposal).
- **Primary ability:** DEX.
- **Secondary ability:** INT.
- **Saving throw proficiencies:** DEX and INT.
- **Armor:** Light.
- **Weapons:** Finesse melee weapons and compact firearms (precise weapon taxonomy still subject to the shared firearms rules).
- **Signature mechanic:** Precision Strike, rather than multiattack or Deadeye's Focus Dice.

**Subclass identities:** Phantom (disguise, stealth, covert infiltration); Blade Dancer (agile melee, evasion, acrobatics); Saboteur (traps, breaching, physical-security sabotage, environmental interference).

## 4. Proposed base-class features (accepted conceptual draft)

### Levels 1–10

- **Precision Strike (1):** Once per turn, add the listed d6 bonus damage to one *qualifying* weapon attack if you have advantage, or if an ally is engaging the target within 5 feet and you do not have disadvantage. Precise firearm eligibility remains subject to the universal firearm rules. This is modeled on Rogue's Sneak Attack, rather than being tied to supernatural powers or Hacks.
- **Expertise (1, 6):** Double proficiency with two selected skills/tool proficiencies at level 1; choose two more at level 6.
- **Cunning Action (2):** Dash, Disengage, or Hide as a Bonus Action.
- **Uncanny Dodge (5):** Use a Reaction to halve damage from an attack that hits you and that you perceive.
- **Evasion (9):** For qualifying Dexterity-saving-throw area effects, take no damage on a success and half on a failure.
- **Urban Traversal (10):** Climb and navigate urban obstacles more efficiently; ignore ordinary, nonhazardous clutter-based difficult terrain while Dashing.

### Levels 11–20

- **Reliable Talent (13):** On an ability check using a proficiency, treat a d20 result of 9 or less as 10.
- **Silent Entry (14):** Improved proficient physical lock, alarm, and obstacle bypass while minimizing signs of intrusion. Electronic intrusion requires suitable tools or Cyberware and is not an innate Ghost Hack.
- **Ghoststep (17):** While using Disengage, move through a hostile creature's space when physically possible without stopping there.
- **Elusive (18):** Attackers cannot gain advantage on attack rolls against you while conscious and not incapacitated.

### Levels 21–30

- **Strike and Fade (21):** After successfully dealing Precision Strike bonus damage, move up to 10 feet without provoking an opportunity attack from the struck target; once per turn.
- **Ambush Reflex (22):** Better defenses against being caught unaware; protection against surprise while conscious. Integrate with approved Foundry/5e surprise handling later.
- **Exploit Opening (25):** Once per Short Rest, apply Precision Strike to a qualifying hit despite lacking advantage or an adjacent ally, provided you do not have disadvantage.
- **Disabling Precision (26):** On a Precision Strike, forgo 2d6 of bonus damage to force a saving throw; on failure, the target loses Reactions until the start of your next turn. DC to be finalized with the common ability mechanics.
- **Evasive Route (29):** Once per round, after a successful Dexterity saving throw, move up to 10 feet without provoking opportunity attacks, if able to move.
- **Slip the Net (30):** As a Bonus Action, attempt to escape a grapple or physical restraint using an appropriate check rather than automatically escaping.

### Levels 31–40

- **Reflex Reserve (33):** Once per Short Rest, activate Uncanny Dodge without consuming your Reaction; no more than once per round.
- **Blind Spot (34):** Additional opportunities to attempt Hide while exploiting partial visual obstruction or dim lighting. Does not create invisibility or allow hiding in a completely exposed, unobstructed view.
- **Perfect Extraction (37):** Strike and Fade movement increases to half your speed.
- **Never There (38):** Once per Long Rest, as a Reaction, turn one otherwise successful attack against you into a miss, provided you can react.
- **Urban Legend (40):** Once per Long Rest, enter an extraordinary combat-awareness state for up to one minute. While active:
  - Apply Precision Strike once per turn without advantage or an adjacent ally, as long as you do not have disadvantage.
  - An enemy hit with your Precision Strike cannot take Reactions until the start of your next turn.
  - Movement following your Precision Strike does not provoke opportunity attacks from that target.
  - This is not supernatural teleportation or invisibility.

## 5. Precision Strike and reserved advancements

**Bonus damage:** 1d6 at level 1; increases by 1d6 on every subsequent odd-numbered Ghost level (3, 5, 7, ..., 39), culminating at **20d6 at level 39–40**. Under the accepted concept the damage applies **once per turn** to one qualifying attack, not once to each attack and not an unlimited number of times on a single turn.

**Ten ASI/feat selections:** levels **4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 28, 32, 36, 40**.  
**Ten subclass milestones:** levels **3, 7, 11, 15, 19, 23, 27, 31, 35, 39**.

The positions are reserved for all three subclasses. **Actual Phantom, Blade Dancer, and Saboteur abilities are not yet designed or approved.**

## 6. Important boundaries / open implementation questions

- The user approved this draft's full progression; exact damage balance and combat DCs still need tuning against firearm damage, enemy HP, and the other five classes for 1–40 play.
- A Ghost does **not** gain an automatic Hack, Cyberware prerequisite bypass, or free optical camouflage just by being a Ghost. Cyberware = hardware, Augmentations = firmware, Hacks = software remains authoritative.
- Universal proficiency progression beyond level 20, ASI/feat rules, ability-score caps, encounter scaling, skill/DC conventions, stealth/perception rules, firearm ranges, and surprise mechanics all require cross-class / system-wide design.
- Feature text above records the approved *draft concepts*, not finished technical rules ready to implement or a claim of game testing.
- Ghost's strengths should come from creating openings, exploiting them, and escaping, rather than frontlining like Vanguard or copying Deadeye's Focus Dice and attack progression.
