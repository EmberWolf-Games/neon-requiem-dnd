# Playable Races — Human and Synth

**Status:** Approved foundation; individual racial traits still to be designed  
**Recorded:** 21 Nov 2026  
**Implementation:** Not started

Neon Requiem replaces the playable race options inherited from 5e. At character creation there are **two choices: Human and Synth**.

| | Human | Synth |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nature | Biological, baseline human | Artificial android or automaton |
| Appearance | Human | Designed to look just like a human |
| Prevalence | Most common population in the setting | Not yet specified |
| Starting Cyberware | **1 installed item** | **2 installed items** |
| Body-owned Augmentation slots | **6** | **6** |
| Racial bonuses and drawbacks | Not yet defined | Distinct benefits and tradeoffs, not yet defined |

## What these choices mean

**Humans** are the baseline population and the most common people in the world. A Human begins play with **one piece of Cyberware already installed**.

**Synths** are artificial beings built to resemble humans closely. A Synth begins play with **two pieces of Cyberware already installed**. Its additional starting hardware is part of a larger racial tradeoff; the owner has established that Synths will have benefits **and** disadvantages compared with Humans, but has not yet assigned the specific traits.

Both races begin with **six body-owned, type/color-coded Augmentation slots**. Their starting Cyberware may provide extra slots **only if the chosen hardware explicitly grants them**. See the [technology foundation](https://github.com/EmberWolf-Games/neon-requiem-dnd/pull/8) for slot ownership, color matching and what happens when granting hardware is removed.

The [Character Identity Creator](Neon_Requiem_GDD_Character_Creator_Animation_Addendum_v2.md) has four initial visual bases: Male Human, Female Human, Male Synth and Female Synth. These are **appearance presets for two playable races**, not four mechanical races. Appearance selections do not silently modify racial stats.

## Not yet decided

- Human and Synth trait bonuses, resistances, vulnerabilities and other pros/cons.
- The catalog of permitted starting Cyberware, selection restrictions and acquisition rules.
- Whether Synth repair, healing and recovery require distinct procedures, and how Lifeline and Ripperdoc interact with those rules.
- Any restrictions on Cyberware fitment, body replacement or racial prerequisites beyond the decisions already recorded.

The approved number of races and the **one/two starting Cyberware** distinction are fixed foundations. Everything else above remains for later design.

## Backgrounds

The owner also decided to **replace all inherited 5e backgrounds**, but no new Neon Requiem background list or background mechanics have been specified. The replacement is a design requirement, not a completed feature.
