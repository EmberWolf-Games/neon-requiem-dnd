# NEON REQUIEM — Vanguard Subclasses (1–40)

**Decision status:** OWNER-PRE-APPROVED SUBCLASS DESIGN DRAFT — mechanics open to future revisions and balancing  
**Date:** 2026-11-21  
**Base class:** Vanguard (owner-approved progression [1–40](Neon_Requiem_GDD_Vanguard_Progression_1-40.md))  
**Milestones:** 3, 7, 11, 15, 19, 23, 27, 31, 35, 39 (Vanguard class levels, not overall character levels)  
**Implementation:** NOT IMPLEMENTED. Owner's pre-approval allows recording subclass designs; it is not permission to change the Foundry game engine or publish a build.

## Shared conventions

- **Class feature DC (provisional):** 8 + proficiency bonus + the relevant Vanguard ability modifier (normally STR; features naming CON use CON). The system-wide proficiency bonus rules beyond level 20 remain unresolved.
- Uses per rest and action costs written below are **initial playtest numbers**, editable in subsequent balance review.
- Subclass progression grants no extra ASI/feat selections. Vanguard's ASI/feat slots remain **4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 28, 32, 36, 40**.
- A feature does not grant new Cyberware, Augmentation slots, Hack capacity, or nonphysical supernatural effects. It builds on core Vanguard Stances, Grit, Combat Overdrive and Intercept.
- Where physical contact, equipment, a visible target, or a reaction is required, those requirements still apply. A feature cannot overcome total cover, immobilization, or lost equipment merely because the character is high level.

## Juggernaut

**Identity:** Absorb punishment, hold terrain, and survive conditions that would break another frontline fighter.

| Vanguard level | Feature | Draft effect |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 3 | **Braced Frame** | Once per Short Rest, when an attack damages you, use your Reaction to reduce that damage by 1d6 + your CON modifier (minimum 1). You must be conscious and able to react. |
| 7 | **Reinforced Brace** | Braced Frame gains a second use per Short Rest. While in Guard or Anchor Stance, you also have advantage on checks to resist forced movement. |
| 11 | **Reserve Plating** | Whenever you use Grit, gain temporary HP equal to your Vanguard level divided by four (round down, minimum 1) after resolving its healing. Temporary HP follows normal nonstacking rules. |
| 15 | **Overdrive Resilience** | Once per Short Rest, when you activate Combat Overdrive, gain resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage until the start of your next turn. This is physical endurance, not immunity. |
| 19 | **Immovable Frame** | While conscious, you have advantage on saving throws against being knocked prone and on ability checks against being pushed, pulled, or grappled. |
| 23 | **Layered Defense** | When Braced Frame reduces damage, it also reduces the next instance of weapon damage you take before your next turn by your CON modifier (minimum 1). No Reaction is needed for this second reduction. |
| 27 | **Fortress Step** | While Guard or Anchor Stance is active, entering a space within 5 feet of you costs a hostile creature 5 additional feet of movement once per turn. This doesn't affect creatures immune to movement impediments or those teleporting. |
| 31 | **Enduring Iron** | Once per Long Rest when you use Iron Resolve, also gain temporary HP equal to twice your CON modifier (minimum 2) whether the reroll succeeds or fails. |
| 35 | **Siegebreaker Survivor** | Once per Long Rest when you take damage equal to or greater than one-quarter of your maximum HP from one source, reduce that damage by your CON modifier + your Vanguard proficiency bonus, after other reductions. |
| 39 | **Unbreakable Core** | Once per Short Rest, you may use Braced Frame without spending its Reaction or one of its uses, but no more than once in a round. This is a defensive mastery, not a second turn. |

## Reaver

**Identity:** Aggressive melee pursuit, high-risk pressure, and brutal execution without copying the Deadeye's precision dice.

| Vanguard level | Feature | Draft effect |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 3 | **Rushing Assault** | Once on your turn, if you moved at least 10 feet toward an enemy before landing a melee weapon hit, deal additional damage equal to your STR modifier (minimum 1). |
| 7 | **Counterforce** | Once per Short Rest, when a creature within your melee reach misses you with an attack, use your Reaction to make one melee weapon attack against it. |
| 11 | **Predator Momentum** | Once on your turn after scoring a critical melee hit or reducing a hostile creature to 0 HP, move up to 10 feet without provoking opportunity attacks from the target you struck. |
| 15 | **Overdrive Impact** | When you activate Combat Overdrive, your first successful melee weapon attack before the end of that turn deals one additional weapon damage die. |
| 19 | **Rupture Strike** | Once per Short Rest after a successful melee weapon hit, force the target to make a Strength saving throw against your class feature DC; on failure it falls prone and its speed becomes 0 until the start of its next turn. No effect if it cannot be knocked prone. |
| 23 | **Relentless Pressure** | If you hit the same creature with two melee attacks during your turn, add your proficiency bonus to one subsequent melee damage roll against it before the turn ends. At most once per turn. |
| 27 | **Violent Follow-Through** | Once per Short Rest, after downing a hostile creature with a melee weapon attack, make one melee weapon attack against a different enemy in reach as a Bonus Action. |
| 31 | **Critical Rhythm** | During a turn in which you use Combat Overdrive, your first melee attack scores a critical hit on a 19 or 20. This does not modify later attacks in the same turn. |
| 35 | **Execution Drive** | Once per turn, when you reduce a hostile creature to 0 HP with melee weapon damage, you may move up to half your speed toward a different visible enemy. This movement can provoke opportunity attacks from other enemies. |
| 39 | **Reaver Supreme** | During Combat Overdrive, once on each of your turns when you hit in melee, deal two additional weapon damage dice. This does not grant additional Overdrive uses or actions. |

## Warden

**Identity:** Shield allies, intercept hostile movement, and anchor a squad's defensive position.

| Vanguard level | Feature | Draft effect |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 3 | **Guardian's Warning** | When an enemy within 5 feet of you attacks an ally other than you, use your Reaction to impose disadvantage on that attack. You must perceive the attack and be able to physically interfere. |
| 7 | **Escort Step** | After you use Guardian's Warning or Intercept, you and the protected ally may each move 5 feet if both can move and remain within 5 feet of each other. This movement doesn't provoke opportunity attacks from that attacker. Once per round. |
| 11 | **Lockdown Strike** | When you hit a creature with an opportunity attack, its speed becomes 0 for the rest of that turn, provided its movement can be stopped by a physical attack. |
| 15 | **Defensive Formation** | While in Guard Stance and within 5 feet of at least one ally, you and one adjacent ally of your choice gain +1 AC against ranged weapon attacks. The benefit ends if physical positioning no longer supports protection. |
| 19 | **Intercept Advance** | When an ally within 10 feet is hit by an attack, you may move up to half your speed as part of using Intercept to reach an adjacent position before reducing the damage. You need an unobstructed path and still spend your Reaction. |
| 23 | **Zone of Denial** | Hostile creatures moving from outside into your melee reach expend 5 extra feet of movement once per turn. This is physical crowd control and does not stop teleports or forced movement. |
| 27 | **Shared Grit** | When using Intercept, you may also expend one available use of Grit to add 1d12 to the damage reduction. This expenditure does not heal you. |
| 31 | **Coordinated Response** | An ally protected by your Intercept gains advantage on its next weapon attack against that attacker before the end of its next turn. Only one ally can benefit per round. |
| 35 | **Guardian Formation** | Once per Long Rest, establish a defensive formation for up to one minute while conscious: allies within 5 feet who can use your cover gain +2 AC against ranged attacks and advantage on checks to resist forced movement. The effect requires plausible physical positioning, not a magical aura. |
| 39 | **Supreme Interceptor** | Once per Short Rest, after you already used Intercept this round, use Intercept once more without spending another Reaction. You still must be able to perceive and reach the protected ally. |

## Implementation / balance notes

The three subclass experiences are intentionally distinct: Juggernaut absorbs incoming damage, Reaver presses an aggressive melee advantage, and Warden protects space and allies. Review overlapping Reaction features against base Vanguard **Intercept**, especially Warden's protective options, and test Reaver's critical-hit/Extra Attack interactions at levels 29–40. Do not treat these untested values as final.

**Approval scope:** All 30 milestone features across these three subclasses are pre-approved design starting points; changes remain explicitly welcome later.
