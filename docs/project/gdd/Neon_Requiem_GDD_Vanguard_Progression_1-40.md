# Vanguard — Base Class Progression (Levels 1–40)

**Status:** Approved design; numerical balance pending  
**Recorded:** 21 Nov 2026  
**Subclasses:** See [Vanguard subclass features](Neon_Requiem_GDD_Vanguard_Subclasses_1-40.md)  
**Build status:** Design only; no game-system implementation yet.

## 1. Class concept and starting proposal

- **Vanguard:** the frontline bruiser/tank, a specialist in physical combat, survival, close-quarters pressure, and protection.
- **5e mechanic references:** Fighter for fighting styles, Second Wind-like self-recovery, Action Surge-like action bursts, Extra Attack; Barbarian for endurance and damage mitigation; Paladin for the *protective martial role* without magic.
- **Class chassis:** d12 Hit Die; Strength and Constitution as the major attributes; heavy-armor proficiency.
- Magic, spell slots, divine abilities, and other traditional spellcasting remain excluded from Vanguard's feature concepts.
- **ASI/Feat** refers to the 5e Ability Score Improvement / feat choice. The final ASI/feat terminology and caps will be set in the shared advancement rules.

## 4. Base-class features

### Levels 1–10

- **Combat Stances (1):** Choose two known stances from Assault (melee pressure), Guard (defense), and Anchor (grappling/displacement resistance). Switch between known stances using a Bonus Action.
- **Grit (1):** Limited-use self-recovery, modeled on Fighter's Second Wind. Restores HP via endurance rather than relying on healing Cyberware.
- **Combat Overdrive (2):** Take one additional action on your turn, once per Short Rest. Only one Overdrive activation on a single turn.
- **Extra Attack (5):** Two attacks with the Attack action.
- **Intercept (6):** Use a Reaction to reduce damage against an adjacent ally.
- **Iron Resolve (9):** Reroll one failed saving throw, once per Long Rest.
- **Pressure Point (10):** Once per turn, a melee hit can force a resisting enemy to be pushed or slowed.

### Levels 11–20

- **Extra Attack (13):** Three attacks per Attack action.
- **Grit Refinement (14):** Extra uses of self-recovery.
- **Combat Overdrive (17):** Two uses per Short Rest, still no more than one activation per turn.
- **Pain Suppression (18):** Temporarily resist physical damage when badly injured; limited use; physical endurance or Synth-equivalent response.

### Levels 21–30

- **Adaptive Stance (21):** Know all three stances; shift the active stance at the start of your turn without spending a Bonus Action.
- **Breach and Advance (22):** Improved close-quarters movement after striking or displacing an enemy.
- **Defiant Recovery (25):** Stronger emergency recovery while seriously injured.
- **Pressure Point II (26):** Stronger knockbacks and the option to knock an enemy prone.
- **Extra Attack (29):** Four attacks per Attack action.
- **Iron Resolve (30):** Two failed-save rerolls per Long Rest.

### Levels 31–40

- **Combat Overdrive Mastery (33):** Gain tactical mobility/repositioning benefits when activating Overdrive.
- **Relentless Pursuit (34):** Improved pursuit of enemies trying to flee your melee reach.
- **Bulwark Presence (37):** Provide cover against ranged fire to nearby allies you physically shield.
- **Last Stand (38):** Once per Long Rest, remain at 1 HP when an otherwise survivable attack would reduce you to 0 HP.
- **Apex Vanguard (40):** Once per Long Rest, enter a combat state for up to one minute; benefit from two stances simultaneously, gain temporary HP each turn, and resist being knocked prone or forcibly displaced.

## 5. Reserved advancement and balance boundaries

- **10 ASI/Feat opportunities:** levels 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 28, 32, 36, and 40.
- **10 subclass milestones:** levels 3, 7, 11, 15, 19, 23, 27, 31, 35, and 39. They alternate subclass selection / major features and improvements; their individual feature tracks are recorded in the linked subclass document and remain open to tuning.
- **Extra Attack milestones:** levels 5 (2 attacks), 13 (3), 29 (4). The accepted concept caps normal Vanguard Attack-action attacks at four through level 40, with later identity emerging from defense, control and versatility.
- **No proficiency-bonus progression or ability-score caps are set here.** These must be designed consistently across all six classes as part of a system-wide 40-level math review.
- **No hard-coded damage dice, DC formulas, exact Grit scaling, Pain Suppression resistance values, Defiant Recovery amounts, or exact action costs beyond the conceptual descriptions already listed.** These require separate mechanics/balance approval.
- Values not specified in this document remain open. Balance and Foundry runtime behavior have not yet been verified.
