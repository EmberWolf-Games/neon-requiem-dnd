# Ripperdoc — Special Hybrid Class (Levels 1–20)

**Status:** Approved working design; procedure and multiclass details pending  
**Recorded:** 21 Nov 2026  
**Entry:** Engineer 5 / Lifeline 5, with ability and tool prerequisites  
**Build status:** Design only; not implemented.

## 1. Entry requirements and class levels

Ripperdoc is an earned hybrid, not a seventh starting class. A character must first develop both Engineer and Lifeline. Ripperdocs specialize in cybernetic surgery, implant installation, biomedical and mechanical integration, patient recovery, and implant diagnostics.

### Minimum prerequisites before taking Ripperdoc level 1

- **Engineer level 5 or higher**
- **Lifeline level 5 or higher**
- **Intelligence 13 or higher**
- **Wisdom 13 or higher**
- Proficiency with the appropriate **medical and engineering tools** (precise names await the project's skill/tool taxonomy)

The earliest possible Ripperdoc class level is **overall character level 11**, after at least Engineer 5 and Lifeline 5.

### Level accounting

Ripperdoc has a dedicated **20-level hybrid track**, and every Ripperdoc level counts toward the **overall character-level cap of 40**.

**Total character level = Engineer levels + Lifeline levels + Ripperdoc levels + any other applicable class levels; total must not exceed 40.**

Example approved design progression: **Engineer 5 / Lifeline 5 / Ripperdoc 20 / 10 more levels distributed between Engineer and Lifeline**. Another possible total level-40 example is **Engineer 15 / Lifeline 5 / Ripperdoc 20**.

- Taking a Ripperdoc level **does not** automatically increase the Engineer or Lifeline class level.
- Ripperdoc levels **do not** independently advance the Rigger/Fabricator/Architect or Trauma Tech/Nanomedic/Combat Responder subclass milestones.
- Ripperdoc has its own 20-level track and no separate subclass choices.
- Exact class-level accounting in Foundry's inherited 5e engine, multiclass prerequisites beyond those listed, HP/Hit Die progression, overall class-level caps and abilities/proficiency scaling require dedicated implementation design.

## 2. Hybrid mechanic: Surgical Protocols

The central feature is **Dual-System Intervention**: specialized Ripperdoc procedures integrate Engineering and Lifeline knowledge and can consume **both** an Engineer **Deployment Charge** and a Lifeline **Treatment Die**.

- Surgical Protocols are medical/technical procedures involving Cyberware, implants and patient condition; they are **not** fantasy spells, ordinary Hacks or software programs.
- They use **previously earned Engineer and Lifeline resources**, rather than requiring a third generic spell-slot-equivalent pool.
- Appropriate **tools, compatible implants, components, surgical facilities, procedures, and time** remain necessary.
- Class features do not conjure hardware, grant equipment the character does not own, or override Cyberware compatibility.
- Not every Surgical Protocol necessarily consumes exactly one of each resource; **specific protocol costs and balancing remain pending**.
- Final rules must specify whether resource pools advance based on each underlying class only or have approved hybrid modifiers. The current design assumes **no automatic advancement** to either base class's resource scaling from taking Ripperdoc levels.

## 4. Class features

### Ripperdoc levels 1–5: Cybernetic Surgeon

- **Integrated Practice (1):** Combine medical and engineering expertise for complex procedures involving cybernetic bodies and implanted hardware. Gain specialized cybernetic surgery proficiency; exact checks require medical/technical rules.
- **Surgical Protocols (1):** Gain specialized combined interventions that can use both a Treatment Die and Deployment Charge.
- **Cybernetic Diagnostics (2):** Identify installed Cyberware, compatibility conflicts, malfunction symptoms, and the specific source-owned Augmentation slots the hardware provides.
- **Emergency Integration (3):** Stabilize a patient experiencing Cyberware-related complications, with suitable treatment equipment.
- **Field Surgery (5):** Perform certain noncritical Cyberware interventions using a properly equipped field workstation, without a permanent clinic. Permanent implants still require suitable conditions and procedure time.

### Ripperdoc levels 6–10: Advanced Implant Specialist

- **Stabilize and Restore (6):** Combine eligible stabilization and temporary hardware intervention into one action at the proper treatment/deployment resource costs.
- **Precision Fitment (7):** Improve surgery accuracy and reduce installation complications; no exceptions to Cyberware prerequisites.
- **Redundant Life Support (9):** Once per Long Rest, use appropriate equipment to stabilize a critically injured patient pending further definitive treatment.
- **Mobile Operating Theater (10):** Prepare a field surgery workspace supporting more advanced operations with materials and preparation.

### Ripperdoc levels 11–15: Master Cybernetic Surgeon

- **Adaptive Reconstruction (11):** Carry out complex Cyberware replacement/integration during downtime while preserving installed hardware compatibility and slot grants.
- **Neural & Servo Calibration (13):** Briefly calibrate compatible implants to improve reliability or function. **Does not add Augmentation slots**.
- **Swift Intervention (14):** Once per Short Rest, use a Reaction for eligible rapid medical/technical response to an implant emergency within reach.
- **Surgical Mastery (15):** Improve reliability and recovery demands for complex Cyberware removal, replacement and installation.

### Ripperdoc levels 16–20: Legendary Ripperdoc

- **Resilience Protocol (17):** Once per Long Rest, provide temporary life-support to an injured patient pending full treatment using specialized equipment.
- **Reconstructive Expertise (18):** Rebuild severely damaged but repairable Cyberware using appropriate parts, workstation and downtime.
- **Second Chance (19):** Once per Long Rest, attempt extraordinary emergency intervention on a medically recoverable critically injured patient. **Cannot revive an already dead creature**.
- **Perfect Integration (20):** Once per Long Rest, enter a precision surgery/technical-response state lasting up to one minute:
  - Once per turn, reduce an eligible combined medical/engineering procedure's cost by **one Deployment Charge OR one Treatment Die**, with a minimum **total resource cost of one**.
  - Better emergency stabilization when Cyberware is damaged or malfunctioning.
  - Once per turn, perform one eligible emergency medical or technical intervention as a Bonus Action instead of an action.
  - Gain enhanced diagnosis/correction of compatible implant malfunctions.
  - Does **not** permit instant permanent implant surgery during combat, automatic healing without proper supplies, or bypassing compatibility.

## 5. Mandatory Cyberware / Augmentation ownership rules

The separately owner-approved technology systems establish:

- **Cyberware = hardware**, **Augmentations = firmware**, **Hacks = software**.
- Each Human or Synth body owns its **six baseline Augmentation slots**.
- Additional color/type-coded slots belong to the **specific installed Cyberware** granting them.
- An Augmentation belongs to the **specific slot where it is installed**.
- When granting Cyberware is **physically removed**, **its slots and the Augmentations slotted there are lost from the character's active configuration**, without affecting unrelated slots.
- **Ripperdoc NEVER overrides slot provenance**, grants phantom body slots, or allows hardware-specific slots to persist after removal.
- Whether a lost Augmentation item is destroyed, detached intact, recoverable, or moved to inventory remains **undecided**. A future Ripperdoc salvage procedure must be explicitly approved separately rather than assumed.
- Temporary Cyberware disabling, replacement, repair and malfunction have not yet been comprehensively defined for this ownership rule.
