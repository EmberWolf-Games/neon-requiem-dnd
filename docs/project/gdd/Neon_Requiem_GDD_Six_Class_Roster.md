# Neon Requiem — Classes and Specializations

**Status:** Approved class roster; feature tables ready for balance review  
**Recorded:** 21 Nov 2026  
**Implementation:** Not started

## Character classes

Neon Requiem uses the 5e rules chassis but replaces the fantasy class roster with six original classes. Each has three subclasses. Base-class progression runs from level 1 to level 40.

The classes take familiar mechanical roles from 5e without importing traditional spellcasting. A Cipher uses installed Hacks, not spells; a Lifeline uses medical equipment, not divine healing. The common technology model remains **Cyberware (hardware), Augmentations (firmware), and Hacks (software)**. See [Technology Systems Foundation, PR #8](https://github.com/EmberWolf-Games/neon-requiem-dnd/pull/8).

| Class | Job in the party | 5e design reference | Subclasses |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Vanguard** | Frontline fighting, endurance, protecting allies | Fighter, Barbarian, Paladin's protective martial role | Juggernaut, Reaver, Warden |
| **Deadeye** | Firearms, precision and heavy weapons | Fighter, Ranger | Longshot, Gunslinger, Demolitionist |
| **Ghost** | Infiltration, mobility and precision damage | Rogue, Monk | Phantom, Blade Dancer, Saboteur |
| **Cipher** | Hacks, electronic warfare and tactical coordination | Wizard/Warlock versatility, Bard-style support, without spells | Intruder, Puppeteer, Handler |
| **Engineer** | Drones, gadgets, equipment and fabrication | Artificer, companion mechanics | Rigger, Fabricator, Architect |
| **Lifeline** | Medical care, healing and extraction | Cleric/Bard support roles, without magic | Trauma Tech, Nanomedic, Combat Responder |

**Operator is not a seventh class.** Its tactical-command role became part of Cipher, chiefly through Handler. Engineer handles machines; Lifeline treats patients. They overlap deliberately in the separate Ripperdoc hybrid.

## Subclass roles

### Vanguard
- **Juggernaut:** Absorbs damage and holds position.
- **Reaver:** Presses melee attacks, pursues targets and counters mistakes.
- **Warden:** Protects allies and restricts enemy movement.

### Deadeye
- **Longshot:** Chooses firing positions and priority targets at range.
- **Gunslinger:** Fights with mobile, close-range firearms.
- **Demolitionist:** Uses heavy weapons, explosives and suppression.

### Ghost
- **Phantom:** Disguise, concealment and clean exits.
- **Blade Dancer:** Fast finesse melee and evasive footwork.
- **Saboteur:** Physical security, traps and prepared disruption.

### Cipher
- **Intruder:** Offensive intrusion and cyberware disruption.
- **Puppeteer:** Control of cameras, drones and other compatible devices.
- **Handler:** Squad communications, targeting and tactical support.

### Engineer
- **Rigger:** Constructs, deploys and controls drones.
- **Fabricator:** Builds, repairs and tunes equipment.
- **Architect:** Cover, turrets, sensors and battlefield construction.

### Lifeline
- **Trauma Tech:** Emergency treatment and critical care.
- **Nanomedic:** Nanotechnology-assisted recovery.
- **Combat Responder:** Treatment on the move and casualty extraction.

## Advancement

Every base class has ten ASI/feat opportunities at **4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 28, 32, 36 and 40**. Each subclass supplies features or improvements at **3, 7, 11, 15, 19, 23, 27, 31, 35 and 39**. The [subclass advancement index](Neon_Requiem_GDD_Subclass_Index_1-40.md) includes all 180 features, with links to each class's full progression and subclass text.

All six base-class feature tables and all eighteen subclass tracks have been designed and provisionally accepted. **Their mechanics remain open to tuning** once the shared firearms, Hack, medical, equipment and level 21–40 rules are settled.

## The special hybrid: Ripperdoc

[Ripperdoc](Neon_Requiem_GDD_Ripperdoc_Hybrid_Progression_1-20.md) is an earned hybrid, **not a starting class**. A character needs at least **Engineer 5 and Lifeline 5**, plus the approved ability and tool prerequisites, before taking Ripperdoc levels. The hybrid has its own **20-level track**, counting toward the character's overall level-40 cap. It has no subclasses.

The hybrid's specialty is cybernetic surgery and integrated medical/engineering procedures; it does not replace either base profession or override Cyberware-owned augmentation slots.

## Rules that still need a common treatment

- Total-level and multiclass advancement, proficiency bonuses, ability-score caps and feat rules after level 20.
- Hack execution, compatibility and resource costs; firearm/ammunition rules; deployable devices and drone command limits.
- Treatment supplies, Synth/Human patient compatibility, damage and recovery scaling.
- Exact class-feature values, edge cases and encounter balance.

The class design is approved as a working specification. **No class or subclass in this document has been implemented or playtested in Foundry.**
