# NEON REQUIEM — GDD Addendum: Six Base Classes and Subclass Roster

**Decision status:** OWNER-APPROVED DESIGN REQUIREMENTS — not implemented  
**Decision date:** 2026-11-21  
**Scope:** The complete base-class and subclass roster, and intended mechanical reference roles for a 40-level game  
**Canonical GDD:** Record as an approved project addendum until the authoritative GDD location/version is confirmed  
**Implementation permission:** None. The owner requested design exploration, one base class at a time, before a finalized level 1–40 feature-table implementation.

## 1. Core character progression rules (approved direction)

- The playable character **class selection from D&D 5e is completely replaced** with original Neon Requiem classes and subclasses; the underlying 5e rules/mechanics are the reference framework rather than the character-option roster.
- Neon Requiem offers **six base classes**, **each with exactly three subclasses** at this design stage (18 subclasses total).
- Classes are being **designed for levels 1–40**, not the usual 1–20. Advancement must leave deliberate progression positions for **ability-score/feat choices** and for **subclass features and improvements**.
- Exact level-by-level class advancement, mechanics, resource pools, ASI/feat cadence, subclass milestone cadence, multiclassing rules, scaling, and balance are **not yet approved**. Develop each class's 1–40 table with owner review.
- All six classes operate in the established **Cyberware = hardware; Augmentations = firmware; Hacks = software** setting, with no traditional fantasy magic. Technology rules are universal unless the owner later adds class-based constraints. See pending [Technology Systems Foundation addendum PR #8](https://github.com/EmberWolf-Games/neon-requiem-dnd/pull/8).

## 2. Approved class roster

| Base class | Primary gameplay identity | 5e mechanical reference / inspiration | Subclasses |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Vanguard** | Frontline tank, melee specialist, protection | Fighter, Barbarian, Paladin's martial/protective elements (no magic) | **Juggernaut**, **Reaver**, **Warden** |
| **Deadeye** | Firearms, precision shooting, heavy weapons | Ranger and Fighter, translated to firearms and tactical combat | **Longshot**, **Gunslinger**, **Demolitionist** |
| **Ghost** | Stealth, infiltration, agility, mobility, precision damage | Rogue and Monk, without supernatural effects | **Phantom**, **Blade Dancer**, **Saboteur** |
| **Cipher** | Hacking, electronic warfare, tactical coordination, information control | Wizard, Bard, Warlock *mechanical roles* translated to software/Hacks and technical support | **Intruder**, **Puppeteer**, **Handler** |
| **Engineer** | Gadgets, drones, fabrication, technical fieldwork | Artificer and Ranger-style companion/device mechanics | **Rigger**, **Fabricator**, **Architect** |
| **Lifeline** | Medicine, healing, trauma response, support, survival | Cleric, Druid, Bard's *healing/support roles* translated to technology, not spellcasting | **Trauma Tech**, **Nanomedic**, **Combat Responder** |

The labels and subclasses above are the owner's accepted revision of the initial six-class pitch. **Operator is not a standalone base class.** Its useful command/information-coordination identity has been absorbed into **Cipher**, especially the **Handler** subclass. **Engineer is no longer the primary healer**; medical healing and bodily recovery support belong to **Lifeline**.

## 3. Class and subclass identities (approved conceptual scope)

### Vanguard — frontline endurance and close combat
- **Juggernaut:** Heavy defensive survivability, mitigating damage, protecting allies.
- **Reaver:** Aggressive melee assaults, pressure, forceful counters.
- **Warden:** Defensive zone control, interception, guarding the squad.

### Deadeye — mastery of ranged weapons
- **Longshot:** Long-range precision, sniping, weak-point targeting.
- **Gunslinger:** Fast, mobile, short-to-midrange firearms combat.
- **Demolitionist:** Heavy weapons, explosives, suppressive fire, area denial.

### Ghost — infiltration, stealth, and precision
- **Phantom:** Covert entry, stealth operations, concealment.
- **Blade Dancer:** Agile close combat, evasion, acrobatic mobility.
- **Saboteur:** Traps, sabotage, breaching, environmental interference.

### Cipher — hacking and information dominance
- **Intruder:** Offensive Hacks, disruption of cybernetics, electronic intrusion.
- **Puppeteer:** Cameras, drones, security networks, connected-device manipulation.
- **Handler:** Tactical communication, coordinated targeting, squad intelligence, command, information warfare and psychological pressure.

### Engineer — devices, fabrication, and robotics
- **Rigger:** Drone operation, remote-controlled equipment, automated support.
- **Fabricator:** Crafting, repairs, equipment/weapon modifications.
- **Architect:** Deployable turrets, barriers, traps, field gadgets, tactical engineering.

### Lifeline — technological healing and support
- **Trauma Tech:** Emergency medical intervention, stabilization, treatment, combat readiness.
- **Nanomedic:** Nanotechnology, enhanced recovery, regenerative treatments, medical Augmentations.
- **Combat Responder:** Mobile field medicine, protective support, rapid extraction.

## 4. Locked boundaries

1. **Retain exactly six base classes.** Do not quietly reintroduce Operator as a seventh class.
2. **Keep Engineer distinct from Lifeline:** the former centers on gadgets/drones/equipment; the latter is focused on medical healing and bodily support. Some practical overlap in tools may be possible, but the class identities must remain distinct.
3. **Keep Cipher distinct from Engineer:** Cipher specializes in software, Hacks, and information/coordination; Engineer specializes in physical devices, engineering, and machinery.
4. **No traditional spellcasting** is implied by the 5e mechanical reference labels; they indicate game-design precedents, not a proposal to grant spell slots or fantasy spells.
5. **No class is implemented or balanced merely because its name, role, or subclass is approved.** Advancement tables and specific abilities remain subject to owner review.
6. Character ancestry (Human/Synth), backgrounds, and the six baseline augmentation slots are separate design systems. Character selection should not silently alter these standing rules.
7. Any future class feature progression must span **all 40 levels** and reserve visible places for ability-score/feat selections, subclass entry, subclass upgrades, and base-class milestones.

## 5. Next design step
