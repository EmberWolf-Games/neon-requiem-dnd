## 1. Class foundation

**Engineer** is the physical-technology expert: gadgets, robotics, equipment modifications, deployable defenses, field repairs, and changing the terrain with machinery. Engineer and Lifeline retain distinct base-class roles; their skills can be combined through the separately approved **Ripperdoc** hybrid class.

- **Mechanical references:** 5e Artificer-style equipment enhancement, Ranger-style companions, and Fighter battlefield tactics, without magic or spellcasting.
- **Hit Die:** d8.
- **Primary ability:** INT.
- **Secondary ability:** DEX.
- **Saving throw proficiencies:** INT and CON.
- **Armor training:** Light and medium.
- **Weapon training:** Simple weapons, compact firearms.
- **Technical tools:** Engineering, electronics and fabrication tools; exact proficiency names pending Neon Requiem skill/tool taxonomy.
- **Signature mechanics:** **Gadget Schematics**, **Deployment Charges**, and physical **Deployables**.

The three approved subclasses are:
- **Rigger:** Drone specialist — reconnaissance, drone companions and remote operation.
- **Fabricator:** Equipment specialist — weapon/armor/tool modification, crafting and repairs.
- **Architect:** Battlefield construction — deployable turrets, barriers, traps and field control.

This class grants technical deployment expertise; it does **not** automatically grant Hacks, Cyberware, extra Augmentation slots, or exceptions to hardware/firmware/software compatibility.

## 4. Conceptual class feature definitions accepted in Engineer v1

### Levels 1–10

- **Workshop Training (1):** Gain appropriate technical tool proficiency and Expertise in one engineering/fabrication proficiency.
- **Gadget Schematics and Field Kit (1):** Learn **two basic Gadget Schematics**. The Field Kit contains tools/components to assemble them. Initially **one basic gadget can be deployed per Short Rest without Deployment Charges**; the charge mechanic starts at level 2.
- **Deployment Charges (2):** Gain **four charges**, restored on a Short or Long Rest. Most class-powered gadget deployments consume **one** charge; maintain **one active deployable** at a time unless a feature says otherwise.
- **Deployable Protocol (2):** Deploy an eligible prepared gadget with an **action**. Physical deployables can occupy space, have durability, and be disabled, damaged or destroyed.
- **Quick Deployment (5):** **Once per Short Rest**, deploy an eligible gadget with a Bonus Action rather than an action; pay the normal charge cost.
- **Emergency Patch (6):** **Once per Short Rest**, emergency repair of damaged equipment or a physically repairable construct. This is distinct from Lifeline's direct patient healing. **Ripperdoc** later combines both areas through earned multiclass prerequisites and surgical features.
- **Targeting Scanner (9):** Use appropriate scanning equipment to identify a physical object, mechanical threat or fortification's structural weakness, improving an appropriate later technical assessment.
- **Modular Tuning (10):** During a Short Rest, reconfigure one prepared gadget's approved property, such as range or durability; gadget-specific modification lists to be designed.

### Levels 11–20

- **Expanded Schematics (13):** Increase prepared schematics from **two to four**; Deployment Charges increase to **five**.
- **Remote Override (14):** Remotely operate eligible owned or authorized deployed equipment within its hardware control range; does not grant unauthorized intrusion into other people's systems.
- **Quick Deployment II (17):** **Two** Quick Deployment uses per Short Rest.
- **Hardened Assembly (18):** Improve gadget physical durability/resistance. Exact device HP/resistance math remains TBD.

### Levels 21–30

- **Dual Deployment (21):** Maintain **two** independent compatible class-created deployables; charges increase to **six**.
- **Improvised Countermeasure (22):** **Once per Short Rest**, use Reaction and suitable equipment to produce a physical defensive countermeasure against a visible threat (example: a temporary barrier).
- **Rapid Reconstruction (25):** **Once per Short Rest**, reconstruct/recover an expended or destroyed class-created device with suitable materials, without paying its usual Deployment Charge again.
- **Adaptive Blueprints (26):** After **one minute** of uninterrupted technical work, exchange one prepared schematic for another known schematic without a Short Rest.
- **Linked Systems (29):** Compatible gadgets share sensor readings and device signals, enabling useful coordinated reactions without automatically adding attacks or actions.
- **Efficient Fabrication (30):** Charges increase to **seven**; conserve more usable materials when repairing/rebuilding personal field devices.

### Levels 31–40

- **Instant Assembly (33):** **Once per Long Rest**, deploy an eligible prepared gadget as part of the same action activating another piece of equipment. Normal costs and active-device limits still apply.
- **Hardened Assembly II (34):** Further improve physical gadget durability and reliability; exact mechanics pending.
- **Autonomous Response (37):** **Once per round**, a compatible active defensive gadget may make one limited programmed reaction to a triggering event without consuming your Bonus Action. Does not imply unlimited offensive attacks.
- **Battlefield Reclamation (38):** Improved post-encounter component salvaging; **once per Long Rest**, recover **one** Deployment Charge after successfully reclaiming appropriate materials.
- **Grand Design (40):** **Once per Long Rest**, activate up to **one minute** of extraordinary field engineering:
  - Operate up to **three** compatible class-created deployables at once;
  - Once per turn, deploy one eligible gadget without spending a Deployment Charge;
  - Command two compatible active devices with a single Bonus Action, within each device's action limits;
  - Temporarily reinforce deployed equipment against incoming damage.
  - Does not materialize devices without components, break equipment compatibility, or permit unlimited turret/drone attacks.

- Deployment Charges in the proposed design recover after a **Short or Long Rest**.
- Charges are **not spell slots**, **not Hack RAM**, and **not Augmentation slots**. They gate class-powered physical gadget use; ordinary equipment remains available to other characters.
- Gadget duration, physical materials, reload/storage, deployment range, action costs, resilience, theft, hacking interactions and total active attack limits require system rules.
- Physical drones and gadgets are not made invulnerable or unlimited by the class feature text.
- HP restoration, Synth/Human medical compatibility and equipment-versus-patient repair interactions remain **not finalized**. The now-approved **Ripperdoc** hybrid introduces integrated medical/technical procedures, without replacing either base class or setting universal patient-repair rules.
- System-wide levels 21–40 proficiency progression, ability-score caps, multiclassing, feats and encounter scaling remain unresolved.

## 7. Approved hybrid addition — Ripperdoc

The owner's deferred Engineer/Lifeline twist is resolved as the **Ripperdoc**: a dedicated, earned special hybrid class requiring **at least five class levels in Engineer and five in Lifeline**, along with proposed ability/tool prerequisites. Ripperdoc supports its own **20-level** advancement track within the **total level-40** cap. See [the approved Ripperdoc GDD addendum](Neon_Requiem_GDD_Ripperdoc_Hybrid_Progression_1-20.md).

**This does not change Engineer's 1–40 base-class feature table.** Engineer does not become a generic healer; Lifeline remains the medical base class; Ripperdoc carries the integration/surgery role. Exact hybrid multiclass logic, resource interactions, implant procedures and universal medical rules remain for separate design/implementation review. No code changes are authorized.
